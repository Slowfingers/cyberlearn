The original asset pages were checked for CC Attribution before import.

Adaptation in CyberLearn: rescaling, rotation and placement in our interactive scene, use of representative physical packages for educational variants, generated inventory renders, PSU stray geometry removal, animation selection, additional labels. Geometry/textures already compressed by the delivery repository are retained. Exact input hashes are in manifest.json. Attribution is distributed here with the models.

Nova, Vector and Atlas specifications are fictional teaching configurations, not manufacturer performance claims. The 990 Pro asset represents its stated 1 TB model; the RTX 3080 FE asset uses its dedicated 12-pin adapter. Original workshop geometry, PCB textures, cable routing and other variant models are authored in CyberLearn.
